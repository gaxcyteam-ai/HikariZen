Our Story

Where Light Meets Zen

Our mission at Hikari Zen is to offer a fine dining experience for those seeking authentic Japanese cuisine — from the everyday enthusiast to the connoisseur of Japan's deep historical food culture. We blend modern convenience with traditional artistry.

Our vision is to become a renowned brand expanding into the broader niches of Japanese culture — sake bars, artisan snack shops, and beyond. We exist to tell the story of Japan, one plate at a time.

Our objective is to create a fine dining experience that illuminates the rich history of Japanese cuisine, making every visit both a meal and an education.

Our Menu

Every dish tells a story — crafted from authentic ingredients with respect for centuries of tradition.

Chef's Pick
Omakase Nigiri Set

📖 Born in Edo-period Tokyo, nigiri was originally street food crafted by vendors for Japan's working class.

Eight pieces of premium hand-pressed nigiri, selected by the chef based on seasonal catch. Tuna, yellowtail, sea bream, and more.

₱2,800 🐟 Fish · 🌾 Gluten
Salmon Aburi Sashimi

📖 Aburi — the art of flame-searing — rose to popularity in the Showa era, marrying tradition with modern technique.

Torched Atlantic salmon over shiso leaf with ponzu gel and tobiko roe. A modern take on an Edo-period classic.

₱1,650 🐟 Fish · 🥚 Eggs
Historical
Wagyu Sukiyaki

📖 Sukiyaki became a symbol of Japan's Meiji-era modernization, when beef was first welcomed into mainstream Japanese cuisine.

A4 Wagyu beef simmered in sweet soy broth with tofu, chrysanthemum greens, and shirataki noodles — a Meiji-era tradition.

₱4,200 🥩 Beef · 🌾 Gluten · 🥚 Eggs
Tonkotsu Black Ramen

📖 Born in Fukuoka's Hakata district in the 1940s, tonkotsu ramen rose from humble stall food to a global phenomenon.

72-hour pork bone broth with black garlic oil, chashu pork belly, soft-boiled marinated egg, and bamboo shoots.

₱980 🐷 Pork · 🥚 Eggs · 🌾 Gluten
Signature
Ebi Tempura Kaiseki

📖 Tempura was introduced by Portuguese missionaries in the 16th century and elevated into a refined art by Edo-era chefs.

Tiger prawns in featherlight batter served with dashi broth, daikon oroshi, and yuzu salt. Rooted in 17th-century Edo cuisine.

₱1,350 🦐 Shellfish · 🌾 Gluten
Matcha Warabi Mochi

📖 Warabi mochi dates back to the Heian period (794–1185 AD), cherished by Japan's imperial court as a delicacy.

Bracken-starch mochi dusted with ceremonial-grade matcha and kinako soybean flour, served with housemade kuromitsu syrup.

₱520 🌿 Soy · Gluten-free option available

The Masters

Our Chefs

World-trained artisans who have devoted their lives to the craft of Japanese cuisine.

Chef Hiroshi Tanaka

Head Chef · Sushi & Kaiseki

Trained for 12 years under a Living National Treasure in Kyoto. Specializes in Edo-mae sushi and kaiseki multi-course dining. His knife work is legendary among peers.

Chef Yuki Matsumoto

Sous Chef · Ramen & Robata

A ramen prodigy from Fukuoka who spent 8 years perfecting tonkotsu broth. Known for his innovative robata grill techniques and exceptional dashi mastery.

Chef Aiko Shimizu

Pastry Chef · Wagashi & Desserts

Certified wagashi artisan from Kanazawa. Blends centuries-old confectionery tradition with contemporary dessert design and the finest seasonal ingredients.
